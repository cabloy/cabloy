## Observed finite proof

- **Identity and gate:** empty drain and close-before-drain preserve one Promise before/after resolution, exact frozen report, synchronous closed admission and zero denied provider/executor/cleanup calls. Provider-triggered and abort-listener reentry see the already-published Promise; repeated close/drain produces one abort. Reentrant owned work obtains but never awaits its own drain.
- **Independent ownership:** controlled provider, work, cleanup, whole-executor continuation and synthetic disposal-observer holds each keep drain pending after cancellation and terminal. The executor case observes synthetic disposal before holding its remaining continuation; the disposal case holds before observation. These are not real failing framework disposers. Release/join in finally permits one normal release under existing rules.
- **Terminal and teardown:** independently delayed response terminal holds ownership. An instance-local teardown witness observes `active === 0` after normal slot release, reenters the same drain and proves its completion handler has not run before outer teardown. No polling framework counters or slot release as settlement.
- **Observation is not settlement:** held provider/cleanup cross the 1-second cleanup observation, fault/close the owner and retain capacity while drain remains pending. Finally-owned release/join yields `faulted` with quarantine 1; no timer pretends settlement or repairs capacity.
- **Persistent faults/rejection:** controlled cleanup faults before/during drain persist. Admitted outer rejection before/during drain preserves caller Error identity with a consumed rejection assertion; local teardown rejection after normal release yields `faulted` with quarantine 0. No raw Error output or detached rejecting chain. This does not qualify real failed-disposer recovery.
- **Explicit exclusion:** two admitted held providers fill capacity; a pre-drain nonadmitted delayed-terminal 429 and post-close delayed-terminal 503 invoke no provider/executor/cleanup. Admitted drain resolves with both denied tasks/listeners still pending. Tests then finish/join them separately and assert observer removal. `drained` is not all-invocation or app-shutdown proof.
- **Regression classes:** original real healthy executor/ALS/disposal overlap passes; original controlled faults and synthetic native doubles remain separately labeled. Source-scope test retains no production registration/SDK/listener.
